# Business-logic gaps: proposed plan (nothing here is built yet)

Source: section 3 of `security-audit.md`. Ordered by how much each matters for a real Umoja election, with a rough size
(S = under a day, M = a few days, L = a week or more) and the decision I need from you before building it.

| # | Gap | Why it matters | What I would build | Size | Decision needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ties** | A tie in a leadership vote has no outcome today; the dashboard only flags it. | Detect a tie for first place at close, show it plainly on the results page and the CSV, and offer "Start run-off" which clones the tied candidates and the voter list into a new campaign (new snapshot, new seal, linked to the first). | M | Your tie rule: run-off, or a casting vote, or a draw. Maximum run-offs. |
| 2 | **Abstain / none of the above** | Members who dislike every candidate either skip the vote or pick one. | A per-campaign option that adds an "Abstain" line to the ballot. Counted like a candidate (same tally table, flagged), reported separately, never counted as a winner, included in turnout. | S | Whether it is on by default, and the wording. |
| 3 | **Co-admins, observers, second approver** | One creator can reset, close early and delete alone; nobody independent can watch. Also the missing half of SEC-04. | A role per campaign: *owner*, *co-admin* (can run it), *observer* (read-only: turnout, sealed result, verify, audit entries; never live per-candidate counts). Permanent delete and reset need a second person (not the actor) to approve. | M | Who may be an observer (HR, union rep, internal audit)? Do reset and delete both need two people? |
| 4 | **Candidate withdraws** | Today the only fix is a reset or a new campaign. | After start: mark a candidate *withdrawn* (stays on the ballot, greyed, cannot be chosen, existing votes for them are listed as void and the voter is told they may re-vote). Before start nothing changes. | M | Policy: do the votes for a withdrawn candidate count, void, or does everyone re-vote? |
| 5 | **Retention and deletion** | No rule says how long ballots, receipts, audit entries, photos and user rows are kept; `users` rows have no deletion path. | A written retention schedule, a scheduled job that deletes archived campaigns' receipts and photos after N months (keeping the sealed result), and removal of personal data for people who have left (name, email, employee code), keeping the account id. | M | The retention periods, and what Namibian data-protection rules require. |
| 6 | **Backup, restore, disaster recovery** | If the database or the single container is lost mid-vote there is no tested way back. | Check and set Neon point-in-time-recovery retention; a copy of every sealed result to the R2 bucket at close; a restore drill (documented, run once); an uptime check and an alert when the app or the Entra token request fails; a one-page runbook. | S to M | Acceptable downtime during a vote, and who gets the alert. |
| 7 | **Notifying staff** | Turnout depends on people finding the QR code. | Email (Microsoft Graph mail) or Teams message at open, a reminder to eligible members who have not voted (the system knows receipts, nobody else sees who), and a closing-soon notice. | M | Channel (email or Teams), sender address, reminder schedule. Needs a Graph mail permission from IT. |
| 8 | **Quorum, scheduled publishing** | A vote with 3 of 400 members is valid today; results publish only when the creator clicks. | Optional minimum turnout (a result below it is flagged), optional publish time, and an "approved by" line when publishing. | S | Whether Umoja's constitution has a quorum. |
| 9 | **Audit export for Umoja leadership** | Leadership gets a CSV of tallies and nothing about how the result was reached. | One downloadable bundle (PDF + CSV): result, turnout against the frozen voter count, every reset and early close with reasons, the sealed fingerprint and how to verify it, the audit chain status. | M | Who receives it and in what format. |
| 10 | **Staff joining or leaving mid-campaign** | The voter list is frozen at start (by design); someone hired or reinstated afterwards cannot vote. | An owner action "add a late voter" (needs a reason, shown in the audit log and the export). Leavers keep their vote; their account stops working when IT disables it. | S | Whether late additions are allowed at all. |
| 11 | **Accessibility and poor signal** | Not tested; the client retries reads but has no offline handling. | A WCAG 2.1 AA pass (keyboard, screen reader, large text, contrast) with automated checks in CI; a clear "your vote may not have been recorded, check My votes" state after a lost response. | S to M | Whether you want an external accessibility review. |
| 12 | **Entra secret expiry: outside monitoring** | The in-app banner only helps if an admin opens the app. | A scheduled check (outside the app) that exercises the token request and alerts before and after expiry. | S | Where alerts go. |

## Suggested order

1. **Ties (1)** and **abstain (2)**: they change what a result means, so they must be settled before the first real campaign.
2. **Co-admins and a second approver (3)**: closes the last gap in the audit for insider abuse.
3. **Backup and recovery (6)** and **monitoring (12)**: mostly operations work, and what would hurt most on the day.
4. **Retention (5)**, **audit export (9)**, **notifications (7)**: needed for a properly run election, not for the first dry run.
5. The rest (4, 8, 10, 11) as the first real campaign shows which matter.
